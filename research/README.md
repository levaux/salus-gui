# Research

Open questions. Nothing here is committed to, scheduled, or built.

## What belongs here

A document belongs in `research/` while its **question is still open** — no decision has been
taken that the thing should be built, and the output is evidence or an option space rather than
a stage ladder. That is the line against the two folders either side:

| | Question | Output |
|---|---|---|
| `research/` | still open | evidence, options, invariants to prove later |
| [`docs/plans/backlog/`](../docs/plans/) | settled in principle, only scheduling is pending | a plan that could be promoted tomorrow |
| [`docs/`](../docs/) | answered and shipped | reference for what exists today |

## The promotion path

**research → `docs/plans/backlog/` → `docs/plans/NNN-*.md` → `docs/plans/completed/`**

When a research question resolves into something worth building, the plan that comes out of it
goes into the backlog and the research stays here as the record of how the shape was arrived at.
See [docs/plans/INDEX.md](../docs/plans/INDEX.md) for the rest of the lifecycle.
